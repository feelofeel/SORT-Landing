SORT shows what needs attention today. Poster still records sales and quantities. Practise three habits during the first shift.

## At the start

1. Open [app.getsort.app](https://app.getsort.app) on the work tablet and allow notifications.
2. On **Today**, find **New batches**.
3. Compare the date and quantity with the packaging. If correct, tap **Confirm**. If not, [correct the batch](/en/guides/confirm-or-correct-batch/).
4. Review orange cards that expire today and red cards that are already expired.

## During the shift

Sell the earliest expiry first. If a card says an older batch exists, find it and place it in front. Record supplies and prepared batches in Poster immediately; otherwise early sales can be attributed to the wrong batch.

## At the end

Open **Today** again. For anything expired or unable to last until the next shift, check the remainder, choose the truthful reason, and tap **🗑 Write off**. Follow [write off a batch correctly](/en/guides/write-off-batch/) for the full procedure.

If nothing expires, there is no extra work. SORT never writes off stock automatically.
