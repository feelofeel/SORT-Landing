Install SORT on the home screen **before the first sign-in**, then sign in inside the app. The session then lives where you will actually work, and notifications about expiring batches reach the tablet.

## Why install first

- **Notifications.** On iPad and iPhone, push notifications reach only an app added to the Home Screen. A regular browser tab does not receive them.
- **Sign-in.** On iOS, a browser tab and the Home Screen app are separate sign-ins. If you signed in inside a tab, you will have to sign in again in the installed app.

## iPad and iPhone

1. Open [app.getsort.app](https://app.getsort.app) in **Safari**. It is the most reliable route; in other browsers, availability and steps depend on the iOS version.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Close the Safari tab and open SORT from the new icon on the Home Screen.

## Android

1. Open [app.getsort.app](https://app.getsort.app) in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install** in the banner at the bottom, or open the ⋮ menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open SORT from the new icon.

## Sign in inside the app

1. In the installed app, enter your work email — the one set in Poster.
2. Open the email. You can read it anywhere, even on a phone.
3. Type the **6-digit code** into the app. It signs you in on the sixth digit. The code is valid for 10 minutes, and a new code cancels the previous one.
4. Allow notifications when SORT asks.

The email also contains a sign-in link. It works too, but it signs you in wherever you open it — for example in Safari rather than in the app. Typing the code is the safer route.

## Check it worked

- The SORT icon is on the Home Screen, and the app opens without an address bar.
- You see **Today** without signing in again.
- If you see **No access yet**, your email is not on the Poster employee list. Ask a manager to add it as the employee's login, then tap **Check again**.
