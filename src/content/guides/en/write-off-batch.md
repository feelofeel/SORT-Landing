1. On an expired batch, tap **🗑 Write off**. If a batch is still within its date but will not last until the next shift (the **Until the next shift** section), or it was broken, spilled, or spoiled before expiry, tap **Manual write-off**.
2. Compare SORT's suggested remainder with the physical amount and adjust it with **−/+**.
3. Choose the truthful Poster reason, such as Expired, Defect, or Damaged.
4. If **Also written off** appears, check or choose the linked ingredients.
5. Confirm the write-off once. For 30 seconds you can tap **Cancel**; SORT does not contact Poster before that window ends.

A full write-off closes the batch. A partial write-off reduces the remainder and keeps the card on its shelf. This action creates the main batch write-off in Poster; configured linked ingredients may also be written off at confirmation or when the batch closes.

If the interface says the outcome is unknown, do not tap again: a manager must check Poster first. If the correct reason is missing, ask the manager to add it in Poster.

After success, the card keeps a receipt for linked ingredients. If you notice a mistake after commit, tap **Report an error**; this creates a manager decision and does not automatically restore Poster stock. Offline, the card says **Waiting to sync** and never pretends the write-off already happened.
