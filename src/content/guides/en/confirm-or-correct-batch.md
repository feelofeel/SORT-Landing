1. On **Today**, open **New batches**.
2. Compare the name, quantity, and date with the physical batch. The date on the packaging takes precedence over SORT's calculation.
3. If everything is correct, tap **Confirm**.
4. If the date is wrong, tap it, enter the correct day and time, and save.
5. If the item is stored elsewhere, choose the correct shelf before confirming.
6. If the card shows **Also written off**, complete every **Required** row. SORT will not confirm the batch without the required ingredient or amount.

For several correct cards, use **Confirm all**. Cards without a shelf or requiring a choice remain available individually.

If you see **Unknown expiry**, enter the package date or remove an item that does not belong on a shelf. The manager receives a decision and can save a rule for the future.

Ordinary confirmation does not write off the main batch in Poster. However, when the manager configures linked ingredients **at confirmation** (for example, beans and a paper filter for a brew), confirmation creates Poster write-offs for those ingredients. You have 30 seconds to **Cancel** before it runs; afterward, the card shows exactly what was written off. Offline, it says **Waiting to sync**, never that the write-off already succeeded.
